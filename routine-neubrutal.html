Before you start

  • Lift from day 1 — no cardio-only phase needed. Use lighter weight for the first 1-2 weeks while form locks in.
  • Leg day is deliberately light and machine-based — no squats, deadlifts, jumping, or plyometrics, given your old leg injury.
  • Get clearance from your orthopedist/physio before adding any real load to that leg — this plan can't replace that assessment.
  • If any leg exercise causes pain or affects your walking afterward, stop it permanently and flag it to your physiotherapist.
  • You will not get bulkier lifting in a deficit — bulking needs a calorie surplus, and you're eating below maintenance.
  • Finishers come after weights, never before.
  • Add weight or 1-2 reps every 2 weeks per lift where pain-free — this is what turns "smaller" into "lean."
